What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

🎣

Don't Take the Bait

Phishing attacks can arrive through emails, text messages, phone calls and fake websites. Learning to recognize warning signs can help you stay safer online.

Common Types of Phishing

Phishing can appear in different forms.

📧

Email Phishing

Fake emails designed to trick users into clicking links or sharing information.

📱

Smishing

Phishing attempts delivered through SMS or text messages.

📞

Vishing

Fraudulent phone calls designed to manipulate people.

🎯

Spear Phishing

A targeted phishing attack aimed at a specific person or organization.

Warning Signs

Be careful when you notice these signs.

⚠️

Urgent Requests

Messages that pressure you to act immediately.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links leading to unfamiliar websites.

👤

Unknown Sender

Messages from unfamiliar or unusual accounts.

🔑

Password Requests

Requests for passwords or sensitive information.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites designed to imitate legitimate services.

📝

Unusual Messages

Unexpected wording or strange requests.

How to Stay Safe

01

Verify the Sender

Check who actually sent the message.

02

Think Before Clicking

Avoid unexpected links and attachments.

03

Use MFA

Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.

04

Report Suspicious Messages

Report suspected phishing attempts through the appropriate channel.

Real-Life Scenario

📩

"Your account requires immediate verification."

Imagine receiving an unexpected message claiming that your account will be restricted unless you verify it immediately.

Instead of clicking the provided link, independently visit the organization's official website or contact them using a trusted method.

🔐 Remember: Don't let urgency make your decision for you.
